Green Chilli Paste To taste

Culinary Method

1

Initial Marination

For marination, combine 1 kg chicken with 1 cup yogurt, 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste, salt, black pepper, lemon juice, and a generous handful of fresh coriander; rest for 10 minutes.

2

Sauté & Simmer

Lightly sauté the chicken in a touch of oil, covered, until the oil begins to separate; stir in half a packet of cream.

3

Aromatic Paste

Grind the masala by blending chilli flakes, coriander powder, cumin powder, cashews, almonds, and whole black peppercorns into a fine paste. Add to the simmering pot.

4

The Finish

Garnish with fresh green chillies and coriander. Serve hot with fresh roti or naan. Enjoy the rich, creamy texture!
